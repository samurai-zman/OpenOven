# OpenOven
An open-hardware microwave oven for enthusiasts of particle accelerators and delicious snacks.

# Design Impetus
Every house I have been in, in the United States, has a microwave oven. Yet the knowledge of these magnificent systems and their functionality requires a master's level of education to begin learning about them. This project aims to be an educational and free design that can be built and understood without an E&M 3 coursework. Additionally, because these appliances lack any information regarding repair or maintenance, most often when they die they are thrown to landfills. So this design also seeks to create a design of mwoven that is repairable and easily accessible.

# Main Components of a MWOven:
1 - The Magnetron - Our waveform generator, amplifier, and frequency selector device.  
2 - Cavity - The Box part that acts as a multimodal waveguide that optimizes standing wave resonance at selected frequency + its harmonics.  
3 - Controller - The PCB that tells what to do, when to do it, and how.
